FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to count stock every day?

No. Stock updates with every sale and receipt. Most venues count key items daily and do a full count weekly or monthly.

Can it handle bar stock in pegs and bottles?

Yes. Spirits are tracked in bottles and pegs, with open-bottle levels and excise registers.

Does it work with a central kitchen?

Yes. Plan production, transfer to outlets and track every movement and cost.

Can I see food cost by dish?

Yes. Plate cost and margin are calculated from your recipes and the latest purchase prices.
