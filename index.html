FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Does Digitory keep billing if the internet goes down?

Yes. The POS keeps taking orders, printing KOTs and generating bills offline, and syncs to the cloud when the connection is back.

Does it work with Swiggy and Zomato?

Yes. Delivery orders arrive in the same queue as dine-in and QR orders, go straight to the kitchen, and deduct stock like any other sale.

Is Digitory GST-ready?

Yes. Taxes are set per item and per outlet, bills carry the right GST breakup, and reports are ready for your accountant. Tally export is supported.

Can I manage several outlets from one login?

Yes. You see every outlet on one dashboard, compare them side by side, and control menus, prices and staff access centrally.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on your menu and the modules you choose. Our onboarding team sets up your menu, taxes, printers and staff, and trains your team before day one.

What hardware do I need?

Digitory runs on standard billing terminals, Android tablets and thermal printers. We will tell you what you can reuse and what you need, before you buy anything.
