FAQ

About Managed Inventory

What is Managed Inventory?

A service where Digitory runs your inventory and recipes in the system. Your team does the physical work on site: receiving, issuing, logging waste and counting. Our team sets up the masters and recipes, checks every entry each morning, closes the month and sends you an owners report.

What does my team have to do?

One inventory person, full time on site, receives goods, issues stock to the bar and kitchen, logs waste, comps and staff meals, and does the weekly and month-end counts, using simple checklists in the Digitory app. No inventory experience is needed; we train them.

What do the owner, chef and bar manager do?

The chef and bar manager share recipe cards once, about 2 hours a week in the first month. The owner approves order lists, decides only on what we flag, and joins a 30-minute review call each month.

Is the Digitory team on site?

We work remotely, through Digitory and WhatsApp, with visits when needed. All physical work, such as indents, issues and stock counts, is done by your team at the restaurant.

How soon will I see results?

Within 30 days your top-selling items have costed recipes, your opening stock is counted and the daily routine is running. Your first monthly owners report arrives on Day 5 of the next month.
