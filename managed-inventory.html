How it works

From set-up to a steady monthly rhythm

  1. Set upWeek 1
    We import your menu, build recipes and sub-recipes, set par levels and connect your vendors.
  2. StabiliseWeeks 2–4
    First full count, opening stock fixed, and the reorder and variance agents tuned to your venue.
  3. WatchEvery day
    AI agents watch consumption, prices and deliveries. Your inventory manager follows up on anything unusual.
  4. Count and reconcileEvery week
    We plan the count, reconcile it against sales and share the variance with your team.
  5. ReviewEvery month
    A food cost report by item and outlet, and a review call with the actions for next month.

FAQ

About Managed Inventory

What is Managed Inventory?

A service where a Digitory inventory manager runs your stock control for you, using Digitory’s inventory module and AI agents. We set up recipes, reconcile counts, check deliveries and review food cost with you every month.

Do I still need a store manager?

Your team still receives goods and does the physical counts. We plan the counts, reconcile them, chase the gaps and tell you what to fix. Most venues no longer need a separate cost controller.

Is it remote or on-site?

Remote by default, through Digitory and WhatsApp, with scheduled on-site visits for counts and audits where you need them. [Confirm on-site cities and visit frequency.]

Can I start with software only and add the service later?

Yes. Many customers start with the inventory module and AI agents, then add Managed Inventory when they open more outlets.

How do I know it’s working?

You get a monthly food cost report with variance by item and outlet, the actions taken, and a review call with your inventory manager.
