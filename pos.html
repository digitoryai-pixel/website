FAQ

Common questions

Can Digitory run offline?

Yes. Billing, KOTs and printing continue without internet. Data syncs automatically when the connection returns.

Which devices does the POS run on?

Windows billing terminals, Android tablets and captain apps on Android phones, with standard thermal printers.

Can I set different prices for different areas or times?

Yes. Set prices by area, outlet, order type and time, including happy hours and weekend pricing.

How are voids and discounts controlled?

You decide which roles can void or discount. Approvals and reasons are logged, and reports show who did what.
