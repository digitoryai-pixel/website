FAQ

Common questions

Can I send offers on WhatsApp?

Yes. Campaigns can go out on WhatsApp or SMS to the segments you choose.

Where does guest data come from?

From bills, reservations, QR orders and feedback. Each guest is matched by phone number.

Is guest data kept private?

Access is role-based and phone numbers can be masked for staff. Guests can opt out of messages.

Do you support cashless wallets for events?

Yes, through Obvvy: guest wallets, top-ups, ticketing, entry and refunds.
