FAQ

About DigiTalk

Is DigiTalk a separate product?

No. DigiTalk is part of Digitory and works on the data already in your account.

Can DigiTalk change my menu or prices on its own?

No. It can prepare a change, but a person with the right access has to approve it, and every change can be undone.

Who can see what?

DigiTalk follows the same roles as Digitory. A manager sees their outlet; an owner sees all outlets. Guest phone numbers stay masked.

Which languages does it understand?

English today. [Confirm additional languages before launch.]
