How every agent works

Three levels of trust

Every agent starts by alerting or proposing. It only acts on its own once it has proven itself, and only within the limits you set.

Alert

Spots something and tells the right person, with the evidence.

“Ravi voided 9 bills worth ₹6,400 tonight, 4× his usual.”

Propose

Prepares the work. Nothing changes until a person approves with one tap, and every change can be undone.

The reorder agent drafts tomorrow’s purchase order; the store manager approves it.

Acts within limits

Does the job itself inside rules you set, and reports what it did.

The booking agent confirms tables of up to 6 on WhatsApp; bigger groups go to the manager.

FAQ

Questions about AI agents

What is an AI agent?

A digital teammate that looks after one job, such as reordering stock or replying to reviews. It watches your Digitory data, spots what needs doing, prepares the work and, with your OK, gets it done.

How is that different from DigiTalk?

DigiTalk is how you talk to Digitory’s AI: you ask a question and it answers. Agents work in the background on their own jobs and come to you when something needs a decision.

Can an agent change things without asking me?

Only if you allow it, and only within limits you set, such as booking tables for up to six people. Everything else is a proposal you approve, and every change can be undone.

Do I need new hardware or software?

No. Agents work inside Digitory, on the data already in your account, and reach you on WhatsApp and in the Digitory app.

Is my data safe?

Agents follow the same roles and outlet access as your team. Guest phone numbers stay masked, and guest data is used only with consent.

Which agents do I get?

All agents run on the Digitory modules you use. If you use POS and inventory, for example, you get the POS, kitchen, menu, inventory and purchasing agents.
