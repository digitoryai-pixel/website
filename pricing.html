AI wallet

Pay only for the work the agents do

Top up in rupees. Each agent action takes a small amount. Idle agents cost nothing, and you approve every change they suggest.

  • Prepaid. First load ₹5,000, then top up ₹2,500, ₹5,000, ₹10,000 or ₹25,000.
  • Warned early. Alerts at 20% and 5% balance, with a one-tap top-up.
  • Never blocks billing. At zero, agents pause and the POS keeps running.
  • Fully visible. A monthly statement shows spend by agent and what each one caught.

FAQ

Pricing questions

Are prices per outlet?

Yes. Every price is per outlet, per year, billed in advance. GST is extra. Groups with several outlets get a quote with volume pricing.

What is the AI wallet?

Pubs, bars and breweries pay a lower subscription and top up a prepaid AI wallet in rupees. Each agent action takes a small amount from it. You see every deduction in a monthly statement.

What happens when the wallet runs out?

The agents pause. Billing, KOTs, stock and reports keep working as normal. You get a warning at 20% and 5% balance, with a one-tap top-up link.

Do restaurants and cafés get AI too?

Yes. Core AI is included in every restaurant plan: the morning brief and up to 5 alerts a day. More agents can run on the same prepaid wallet.

Is hardware included?

No. We tell you what you can reuse and what to buy before you spend anything. Hardware purchase, installation and configuration stay with you.

Cloud or on-premise?

Both. Cloud suits most venues. Hybrid, with a local server on site, is recommended for large properties.
